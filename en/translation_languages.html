Freelance Translation & Editing

I work as a freelance translator from English and German into French, with a particular focus on editing and proofreading. My approach prioritizes accuracy, natural tone, and the kind of fluency that makes a translated text read as if it had been written directly in the target language.

As a freelance translator, I have worked with Saas-Fee Tourismus on their destination magazine 4545, many global IT companies, several retailers, and numerous translation agencies — some occasionally, others on an ongoing basis. I also maintain an active profile on ProZ.com, the leading professional network for translators worldwide.
